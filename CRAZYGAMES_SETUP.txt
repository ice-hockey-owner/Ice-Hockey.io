CRAZYGAMES BASIC LAUNCH SETUP

1. Client SDK: CrazyGames SDK v3 (crazygames-sdk-v3.js) is included in index.html.
2. Basic Launch is configured without ad calls. Rewarded-ad rewards are disabled and are not granted.
3. Online server: https://ice-hockey-server.vercel.app
4. Upload the client package contents to CrazyGames. Do not include server secrets or node_modules.
5. Test multiplayer, audio, profile persistence, and controls in the CrazyGames preview before submission.
